A useful number, not a promise

This calculator is a planning model, not financial advice. Returns, dividends, rents, and withdrawal rates are not guaranteed. For an inflation-aware plan, use a real rate (after inflation) and a rate that is net of taxes, fees, vacancies, and recurring property costs as applicable. A 4% portfolio withdrawal rate is not the same thing as a 4% dividend yield. The estimate assumes your selected rate can sustainably fund the spending level you entered; it does not model market volatility, changing expenses, one-time purchases, or a cash reserve.
